Before you start

Please read the terms of use. Choosing “I agree” means you accept them.

  • Sinko is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). It comes as it is, without any warranty: the project is not responsible for what a child can still reach, or for any loss from using it.
  • It is a strong everyday filter, not a lock. A phone on mobile data or with a VPN app does not use this box, and some apps can get around it. Use it together with Screen Time or Family Link.
  • Use it only on your own home network, for devices you look after.
  • Your children’s names, devices and rules stay on this box and are never sent to the project. The privacy statement says exactly what is sent, and where.
